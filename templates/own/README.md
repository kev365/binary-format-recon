# Our templates

Templates generated from this skill's own findings (`tplgen.py`), or
extended copies of imported ones (note the lineage in a header comment).

Everything here starts as status `draft`. Promotion to `complete` happens in
`templates/curation.json`, only after the template has been applied to
samples outside the development corpus (Phase 7). Regenerate the index with
`python scripts/tpl_index.py --write` after adding or promoting anything.

Unlike `templates/imported/`, files here are ours: MIT-style reuse is fine,
but a template derived from a GPL-3.0 import inherits its licence — check
`templates/imported/PROVENANCE.json` before copying.
